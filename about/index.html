About me

I study Industrial Engineering and Management with a focus on artificial intelligence and data-driven systems. I am completing my studies at NYU and preparing my Master’s thesis at LUT in Spring 2026, with the goal of working closely with driven people in a fast-growing environment on a high-impact problem.

I previously co-founded Evolvize Oy, a project that did not move forward the way we hoped, and we decided to stop it. After that I co-founded Nexlead Nordics Oy, where I now work on 360° outbound B2B sales and help Finnish industry leaders reach their ideal clients.

I have been active in Finnish entrepreneurship societies. I joined LUT Entrepreneurship Society as an active team member and later served as President in 2024. I also took part in Aaltoes Ignite in 2023. I'm still active in LUTES.

I enjoy nature, good sushi, hiking and training for ultramarathons.
